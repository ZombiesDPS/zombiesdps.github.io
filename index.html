How it works

Zombies, single target: every shot hits one zombie, with the attachments, range, headshot share and perks set below.

Multi target: total damage per second against 40 zombies in a 5-by-8 grid, the crowd DPS on each weapon card. It uses a fixed reference build and all perks; of the settings below only the headshot share changes it. Weapons without a value are listed as Not ranked.

Bosses: damage per second against the chosen boss during a vulnerable phase, using the range window below. Boss DPS uses its own fixed build and perks; each card explains the details. The columns are weak-spot hits, body hits and the weak-spot share set here.

Attachments

How it works

The chart automatically shows each gun's best DPS setup. It only uses the attachment slots you have ticked: for every ticked slot it picks whatever gives the most DPS, or nothing if no attachment helps. So you choose which slots you want the optimal attachments for – untick a slot to leave it out.

Prestige covers weapon-prestige attachments. Conversion kits are the official kits from weekly or event rewards, now also in the Armory. An attachment that belongs to more than one group needs all of them ticked. Suppressor only is the one setting that forces something: every gun must carry a suppressor (built-in ones count), everything else is still optimised.

Want to set up a gun your own way? Use the Gun Builder ("Build your own" on the weapon card).

How it works

To show how a weapon performs not just up close, the chart does not use only its first damage range. It shows the average damage per second across a range window. Not every distance in that window matters equally, so some parts count more than others. The higher the curve, the more that distance counts. Damage ranges assume that the game's long-range bonus is not active in Zombies; this is not confirmed yet.

The presets stand for different situations. The curve can also be shaped freely, or the window set to fixed distances.

Share of headshots in "Mixed DPS" column

How it works

Head: every bullet hits the head. Body: every bullet hits the body, 45 % chest, 40 % belly and 15 % spread over arms and legs. Mixed: the slider sets how many of them land on the head. Neck shots are not considered for now.

Fire rate cap
How it works

Most people cannot click faster than about 350 to 450 RPM. A semi automatic weapon whose full fire rate is above the set value is calculated at the set value. Binary triggers are exempt.
